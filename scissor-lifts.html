JLG serial-specific equipment information

Open JLG’s official guest lookup. The four reviewed examples returned ship, in-service and warranty dates; two also returned a Product Variant Code (PVC). No manufacture date was established. These fields must not be relabelled as year built.

For example, ES1932 serial M200085546 shipped on 2023-11-09 and entered service on 2024-01-08. RT4069 serial M200087696 shipped on 2024-02-16 and entered service on 2025-02-27, with PVC 2204. Manufacture years remain unknown for both. Read the exact-unit evidence and limitations.
